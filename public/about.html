About me

Computer science graduate, builder, and problem solver with interests spanning firmware, systems software, and technical tools.

Academic & Technical background

I graduated from Montana State University in May 2026 with a B.S. in Computer Science. My academic work included compiler and embedded systems design, computer architecture, cybersecurity practices, and software testing.

My most independent technical projects include "White Lightning", a physical embedded controller and custom PCB, and "CatScript", an independently developed compiler. I also direct and validate AI-assisted software experiments such as FiniteWorlds and ServUP, distinguishing them from my other projects due to their AI use.

Professional experience

In aviation operations, I've worked in ground service and team-lead roles and transitioned into training content development. That experience shaped my attention to procedures, technical communication with stakeholders, reliability, and coordination across disciplines.

What interests me

Embedded systems and firmware, systems software, technical user interfaces, and AI-assisted software experiments. I particularly enjoy problems that require understanding how software interacts with hardware or an existing system's underlying behavior.
