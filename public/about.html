About me

While I'm still figuring out exactly where I want to take my career, my wide range of hobbies and passions have made me someone who loves learning new things, picks up new skills quickly, and is always looking for the next adventure.

Academic & Technical Background

I graduated from Montana State University in May 2026 with a B.S. in Computer Science. My academic work included compiler and embedded systems design, computer architecture, cybersecurity practices, and software testing.

My most independent technical projects include "White Lightning", a physical embedded controller and custom PCB, and "CatScript", an independently developed compiler for my capstone project.

Professional Experience

In 2025, while attending Montana State University, I began working for Horizon Airlines, a regional carrier for Alaska Airlines. In this role I was involved in day-to-day airport operations, eventually being promoted into a temporary leadership position until ultimately transferring back home to Seattle as a Training Content Developer. In this role I work with a diverse range of workgroups from operations to regulatory agencies to implement and maintain technical training programs for airline operations agents.

What Interests Me

Embedded systems and firmware, systems software, web development, and anywhere I can let my creativity loose. I particularly enjoy problems that require understanding the interaction between hardware and software, and any problem that requires creative solutions.
