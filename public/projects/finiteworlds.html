Project 03 / AI-assisted development

FiniteWorlds.

Designing a finite, preplanned Minecraft world-generation system.

JavaFabricRequirementsManual validationAI-assisted
∿ ∿ ∿

The vision

FiniteWorlds explores a modular approach to finite Minecraft worlds in which large-scale terrain and geographic features are planned ahead of runtime chunk generation. The design targets coherent coastlines, terrain, climate, hydrology, and biomes.

My actual role

I created the initial Fabric project foundation, defined the requirements, directed architectural decisions and feature priorities, and manually validated generated outputs. Most of the Java implementation and algorithmic code was generated with AI assistance, and I do not claim independent authorship or comprehensive understanding of its internal implementation.

Iteration and testing

I used preview outputs and in-game exploration to identify artifacts and guide refinements. Work has addressed river flow, terrain morphology, coastline transitions, mountain forms, and runtime-generation behavior. The project is experimental and in development.
